### 🚀 About me

I'm a software engineer who went from full-stack web work to **owning distributed, multi-tenant backend
systems end-to-end** — and I now build at the frontier of **AI-augmented, agentic software development**.
I care about correctness under concurrency, safe rollouts, and using AI to move faster *without* lowering
the quality bar.

<!-- ══════════════════════ WHAT I DO ══════════════════════ -->
### 🧠 What I do now

- 🏗️ **Distributed backends** — design and own multi-tenant services on **.NET 10**, **Orleans** virtual
  actors, and **Marten/Postgres** event-sourced state, plus their **Angular 20 (signals)** front-ends.
- 🤖 **Agentic & AI-augmented engineering** — multi-agent orchestration, LLM-driven development workflows,
  and adversarial AI code review; dozens of small, dependency-ordered changes with structured decision
  records.
- 🚦 **Production discipline** — TDD by default, feature-flagged **dark rollouts**, and multi-tenant safety
  as a first-class review lens.
- 🧪 **Quality automation** — Playwright e2e at scale, custom CI validators, and CI/CD on GitHub Actions.
- 🌱 **Foundations** — research in conformal-prediction data visualization; a trail of GenAI and full-stack
  side projects.
